What happens

  1. 01

    Your wallet signs

    The connected wallet authorizes the blob namespace; Vessel never receives a seed phrase.

  2. 02

    A contract issues the receipt

    Aptos sends the Vessel contract fee to its vault, separate from APT + ShelbyUSD protocol costs. Solana deposits testnet USDC into the Vessel Program vault.

  3. 03

    Shelby stores

    The registered bytes are written under your native or derived Aptos storage address.
