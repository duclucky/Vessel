TRUST THE RECEIPT

Settlement belongs in contracts.

Vessel does not route service payments to an ordinary receiving wallet. Each supported chain has its own governed contract vault.

  • Ed25519-signed quotesWallet, file, retention, price, and expiry are locked before approval.
  • Contract-issued receiptsAptos Move or the Solana Program authorizes the Vessel service flow.
  • Multisig-controlled vaultsAn Aptos Multisig Account and Squads govern the two service-fee vaults.
  • Separated protocol costsShelby storage charges and network gas remain distinct from the Vessel service fee.
